Assessments are structured evaluations that may be timed and/or proctored. Here's how to navigate them successfully.

## Before You Start

### Check Requirements

Before starting an assessment:

- ✅ Stable internet connection
- ✅ Enough time to complete (check time limit)
- ✅ Quiet environment (if proctored)
- ✅ Webcam working (if proctored)
- ✅ Browser up to date

### Consent & Setup

If proctoring is enabled:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept Terms">
    Read and agree to proctoring consent.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Grant Permissions">
    Allow camera and microphone access when prompted.
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/players/camera-permission.png" alt="Camera Permission" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="System Check">
    Verify your camera and mic are working.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Environment Check">
    Ensure good lighting and minimal background distractions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the Assessment

### Navigation

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/players/assessment-interface.png" alt="Assessment Interface" />
</Frame>

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Question List** | See all questions, jump to any |
| **Timer** | Remaining time (top right) |
| **Flag Button** | Mark questions to revisit |
| **Previous/Next** | Navigate between questions |
| **Submit** | Final submission button |

### Answering Questions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Multiple Choice">
    - Click on your chosen answer
    - Only one selection allowed
    - Click again to change
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Multiple Select">
    - Click all that apply
    - Click again to deselect
    - Check question for "select all"
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Short Answer">
    - Type your response
    - Check character/word limits
    - No formatting needed
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Essay">
    - Use the text editor provided
    - Basic formatting available
    - Save periodically (auto-save enabled)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Flagging Questions

Not sure about an answer?

1. Click the **Flag** icon 🚩
2. Continue with other questions
3. Return to flagged questions later
4. Unflag when resolved

### Time Management

<Warning>
Watch your time! When time expires, your current answers are automatically submitted.
</Warning>

Tips:
- Don't spend too long on hard questions
- Answer all questions, even if unsure
- Leave time to review flagged questions

## Proctoring Guidelines

If proctoring is enabled, follow these rules:

### Do ✅

- Keep face visible at all times
- Stay in camera frame
- Maintain eye contact with screen
- Work independently

### Don't ❌

- Switch tabs or windows
- Use unauthorized materials
- Have others in the room
- Cover your camera
- Use a phone

<Info>
Suspicious activity may be flagged for review. Flags don't automatically fail you—a human reviewer checks them.
</Info>

## Submitting

When you're ready to submit:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Answers">
    Use the question list to check all responses.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Check Flagged Questions">
    Resolve any flagged items.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Confirm your final submission.
    
    <Warning>
      Once submitted, you cannot change answers.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    You'll see a confirmation screen with:
    - Submission time
    - Completion status
    - Next steps (if any)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Submission

Depending on settings:

- **Immediate results**: See your score right away
- **Delayed results**: Check back later or wait for email
- **No results**: Score shared only with administrator

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lost connection during assessment" icon="wifi-slash">
    - Your answers are saved automatically
    - Reconnect and resume
    - Timer continues while disconnected
    - Contact support if you can't reconnect
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Camera stopped working" icon="video-slash">
    - Try refreshing the page
    - Check browser permissions
    - Close other apps using camera
    - Contact proctor/support
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Time ran out" icon="clock">
    - Your answers were auto-submitted
    - No additional time given
    - Contact administrator if there was a technical issue
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Accidentally submitted" icon="exclamation">
    - Submissions are final
    - Contact your administrator immediately
    - Explain the situation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Accessibility

Wovser.AI supports:

- **Screen readers**: Compatible with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver
- **Keyboard navigation**: Full keyboard support
- **Extra time**: Request through your administrator
- **High contrast**: Enable in browser settings

<Info>
If you need accommodations, contact your administrator before the assessment.
</Info>

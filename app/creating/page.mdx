## Creator Dashboard

Your dashboard provides quick access to:

- **Recent Sessions** - Quick access to your latest activities
- **Question Bank** - Your library of questions
- **Analytics** - Performance insights
- **Settings** - Organization and personal preferences

<Callout type="info">
  Pro tip: Pin frequently used quizzes to your dashboard for quick access!
</Callout>

## Getting Started as a Creator

1. **Build your question bank** - Start by creating or importing questions
2. **Create your first quiz** - Organize questions into a cohesive assessment
3. **Invite learners** - Share session codes or integrate with your LMS
4. **Review results** - Use analytics to improve future assessments

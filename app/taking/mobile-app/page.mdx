# Mobile App

Take assessments and join quiz sessions on the go with the Wovser.AI mobile app.

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

## Overview

The Wovser.AI mobile app gives you full access to your assessments, quizzes, and results from your phone or tablet. Available for iOS and Android, the app is optimized for on-the-go learning and assessment taking.

## Features

### Assessment Taking
- Browse and search available assessments
- Complete quizzes with a mobile-optimized interface
- Timer and progress indicators
- Submit answers and see results instantly

### Live Quiz Games
- Join live sessions by entering a PIN code
- See questions and answer in real-time
- Watch the leaderboard update live
- Compete with other participants from anywhere

### Results & Progress
- View your scores and detailed feedback
- Track your progress across all assigned content
- See your certificates and badges
- Review past submissions

### Push Notifications
- Get notified when new assessments are assigned
- Receive reminders for upcoming deadlines
- Know immediately when results are released

### Camera Access
- Record video assessment responses directly from the app
- Use your phone's camera for proctored sessions

## Getting Started

<Callout type="info">
  The app uses your existing Wovser.AI account. Sign in with the same credentials you use on the web.
</Callout>

1. Download the Wovser.AI app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)
2. Sign in with your existing account, or use social login (Google, Apple)
3. Browse your assigned assessments or enter a live session PIN
4. Start learning and demonstrating your skills

## Supported Authentication

- Email and password
- Google OAuth
- Apple Sign-In

## Tips

- **Enable push notifications** — Never miss an assignment or deadline
- **Use Wi-Fi for video** — Video assessments upload large files; use a stable connection
- **Find a quiet spot** — For video assessments, record in a quiet, well-lit environment
- **Keep the app updated** — We regularly add new features and improvements

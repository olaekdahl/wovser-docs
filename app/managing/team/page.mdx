# Team Management

Invite team members, assign roles, and control who can access what across your organization.

import { Callout, Steps } from 'nextra/components'

## Roles

Wovser.AI uses four roles to control access within your organization:

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Full access to everything, including billing, settings, and member management. Can delete the organization. |
| **Admin** | Manage content, projects, settings, and integrations. Cannot access billing or modify Owners/other Admins. |
| **Member** | Create and manage content within assigned projects. Cannot access organization settings or billing. |
| **Learner** | Take assessments and view their own results. No creation or management access. |

<Callout type="info">
  Every organization has at least one Owner. The person who creates the organization is automatically the Owner.
</Callout>

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>

### Open Team Settings

Navigate to **Settings** → **Team Members** in your dashboard.

### Click Invite Members

Click the **Invite members** button to open the invitation form.

### Add Invitees

For each person you want to invite, enter their **email address** and select a **role** (Admin, Member, or Learner).

You can add multiple invitations at once, or use **Import CSV** to bulk-invite from a file with the format:

```
email,role
jane@example.com,MEMBER
bob@example.com,LEARNER
```

### Add a Message (Optional)

Include a custom message that will appear in the invitation email (up to 500 characters).

### Send Invites

Click **Send invites**. Each invitee receives an email with a link to join your organization. New users get an account created automatically with passwordless sign-in.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Invitations expire after **7 days**. You can resend an invitation from the team members list if it expires.
</Callout>

## Managing Members

From the **Team Members** page, you can filter and search your team by role, status, name, or email.

### Member Actions

Click the menu button on any member row to access these actions:

| Action | When Available | What It Does |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Change role** | Active or invited members (not Owners) | Assign a different role |
| **Resend invite** | Invited members only | Send a new invitation email |
| **Suspend** | Active members (not Owners) | Temporarily revoke access |
| **Reactivate** | Suspended members | Restore access |
| **Remove** | Any member (not Owners) | Permanently remove from organization |

<Callout type="info">
  Admins cannot modify other Admins or the Owner. Only the Owner can change an Admin's role or remove them.
</Callout>

## Member Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Invited** | Invitation sent, waiting for the person to accept |
| **Active** | Member has accepted and can access the organization |
| **Suspended** | Temporarily locked out — cannot log in until reactivated |
| **Removed** | No longer part of the organization |

## Permission Details

Here's a detailed breakdown of what each role can access:

| Feature | Owner | Admin | Member | Learner |
|---------|-------|-------|--------|---------|
| Create quizzes & assessments | Yes | Yes | Within projects | No |
| Create video assessments | Yes | Yes | Within projects | No |
| Create code challenges | Yes | Yes | Within projects | No |
| View all submissions | Yes | Yes | Within projects | Own only |
| Manage question bank | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Run live sessions | Yes | Yes | Within projects | No |
| Take assessments | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Manage team members | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Organization settings | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Integrations & SSO | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Billing & quotas | Yes | No | No | No |
| Delete organization | Yes | No | No | No |

## Tips

- **Start with Learner role** for assessment takers — you can always upgrade their role later
- **Use Member role** for content creators who only need access to specific projects
- **Reserve Admin** for trusted team leads who need to manage settings and integrations
- **Bulk import** saves time when onboarding large groups — prepare a CSV with emails and roles

## Key Features

### Interactive Quiz Sessions
Run real-time quiz sessions where participants compete live. Perfect for team building, training assessments, or educational environments.

### Technical Assessments
Create comprehensive skill assessments with multiple question types, time limits, and automated scoring.

### Live Coding Challenges
Evaluate programming skills with browser-based VS Code environments. Supports 17 languages including JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, Go, Rust, C++, C#, Ruby, and SQL. Web framework challenges (React, Vue, Angular, Svelte) include live preview URLs.

### Video Assessments
Evaluate communication and presentation skills with recorded video responses. AI-powered transcription automatically scores answers based on keyword matching and speaking analytics.

### Course Wizard
Upload training materials or course documents and let AI generate a complete suite of assessments — quizzes, code challenges, and video assessments — tailored to your content.

### Certificates & Badges
Award professional certificates and digital badges when learners pass assessments. Five template layouts, custom branding, QR verification, and one-click LinkedIn sharing.

### Question Bank
Build a reusable library of questions organized by category, difficulty, and tags. Create once, use across all your assessments.

### Mobile App
Learners can take assessments, join live quiz sessions, and view results from the Wovser.AI mobile app for iOS and Android.

### Team Management
Invite team members, assign roles (Owner, Admin, Member, Learner), and control who can create content, manage settings, or take assessments.

### AI-Powered Question Generation
Leverage AI to generate relevant questions based on your requirements, saving time while maintaining quality.

### Enterprise Integrations
Connect with your existing LMS (Canvas, Moodle), SSO providers (SAML, OIDC), HR systems (Workday, BambooHR, Greenhouse, Lever), and custom systems via webhooks.

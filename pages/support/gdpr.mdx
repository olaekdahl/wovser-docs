# GDPR & Data Rights

Your data, your rights. Wovser.AI provides tools for data access, export, and deletion in compliance with GDPR.

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

## Your Rights

Under GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and similar privacy laws, you have the following rights:

### Right to Access (Article 15)
You can request a summary of all personal data we store about you. Navigate to **Account Settings → Privacy → Data Summary** to see:

- Your account details (name, email, creation date, last sign-in)
- Number of organization memberships
- Number of project memberships
- Assessment submissions count
- Code submissions count
- Share links created

### Right to Data Portability (Article 20)
You can export all your data in a machine-readable format. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Export My Data** to download a complete archive including:

- Profile information
- Assessment submissions and scores
- Code challenge submissions
- Video assessment responses
- Quiz participation records
- Certificate records

<Callout type="info">
  Data exports are generated as JSON files. The export process may take a few minutes depending on the amount of data.
</Callout>

### Right to Erasure (Article 17)
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data. Navigate to **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. All your data — including submissions, scores, certificates, and history — will be permanently removed. You must type "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" to confirm.
</Callout>

#### What Gets Deleted
- Your user account and profile
- All assessment and quiz submissions
- All code challenge submissions
- All video assessment recordings
- Certificate records
- Project and organization memberships
- Notification history

#### What We Retain
- Anonymized analytics data (no personally identifiable information)
- Audit logs required by law (stripped of personal identifiers)

### Right to Object
Contact your organization's administrator or our support team to exercise additional rights such as restricting processing or objecting to specific data uses.

## For Organizations

Organization administrators should be aware that:
- Learner data deletion is the learner's right — they can exercise it at any time
- Audit logging of deletions is maintained for compliance
- Data export includes only the requesting user's data, never other users'

## Contact

For data protection inquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer at the email listed in your account settings, or reach out via [Support](/support/contact).

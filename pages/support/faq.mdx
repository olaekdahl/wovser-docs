## General Questions

### What is Wovser.AI?

Wovser.AI is a comprehensive skills assessment platform that enables organizations to create interactive quizzes, self-paced assessments, and live coding challenges. It's designed for hiring, training, education, and certification use cases.

### Who uses Wovser.AI?

- **HR Teams** - Technical hiring assessments
- **Educators** - Student evaluations and interactive learning
- **Training Departments** - Employee skill validation
- **Certification Bodies** - Credential verification

### What makes Wovser.AI different?

- **AI-powered question generation** — Create assessments faster
- **Live coding environments** — Real terminal access in the browser for 17+ languages
- **Video assessments** — AI-scored video responses with transcription and keyword matching
- **Course Wizard** — Upload course materials and auto-generate full assessment suites
- **Certificates & badges** — Professional credentials with LinkedIn sharing and QR verification
- **Enterprise integrations** — SSO, SCIM, LMS, HR/ATS support
- **Real-time competitions** — Engaging live quiz experiences
- **Mobile app** — Full assessment experience on iOS and Android

## Using Wovser.AI

### How do I join a session?

Go to [app.wovser.ai/join](https://app.wovser.ai/join) and enter the 6-character session code provided by your host.

### Can I pause an assessment?

It depends on how the host configured the assessment. If pausing is enabled, you'll see a **Pause** button. Your progress is saved automatically.

### What happens if I lose internet connection?

Your progress is automatically saved. Reconnect and return to the session—you'll continue from where you left off.

### Can I retake an assessment?

This depends on the assessment settings. If multiple attempts are allowed, you'll see a **Retake** button after completion.

## Video Assessments

### What are video assessments?

Video assessments let you ask questions that learners answer by recording short video responses. Each response is automatically transcribed using AI speech-to-text, analyzed for keyword matches, and scored based on criteria you define. See [Video Assessments (Hosts)](/hosts/video-assessments) for details.

### How is video scoring done?

The AI transcribes the response using speech-to-text (OpenAI Whisper), then matches keywords and synonyms in the transcript against the keywords you defined. Each keyword has a weight, and the overall score is the weighted average across all keyword categories. Critical keywords must be mentioned to pass.

### What about video quality and bandwidth?

Video recording works in any modern browser with camera/microphone access. Videos are uploaded to cloud storage and processed asynchronously. A stable internet connection is recommended, especially for longer recordings.

## Certificates & Badges

### Can learners earn certificates?

Yes! Hosts can create custom certificate templates with their organization's branding. When learners pass an assessment, they automatically receive a certificate they can download as PDF or share on LinkedIn.

### How does certificate verification work?

Each certificate includes a unique verification URL and QR code. Anyone can verify the certificate's authenticity by visiting the link or scanning the code.

## GDPR & Privacy

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Navigate to **Account Settings → Privacy → Export My Data** to download a complete archive of your personal data in JSON format, including all submissions, scores, and certificates.

### Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**. This permanently removes all your data. You must type "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" to confirm. See [GDPR & Data Rights](/support/gdpr) for full details.

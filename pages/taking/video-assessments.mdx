# Video Assessments

Record video responses to questions using your camera and microphone. Your answers are automatically transcribed and scored.

import { Callout, Steps } from 'nextra/components'

## What to Expect

In a video assessment, you'll be presented with one or more questions and asked to record a short video response for each. There are no multiple-choice options — you answer by speaking on camera.

After you submit, AI transcribes your response and checks for key topics and terms that the assessor defined.

## Taking a Video Assessment

<Steps>

### Open the Assessment

Click the invitation link or navigate to the assessment from your dashboard. You'll see the assessment title, description, and number of questions.

### Allow Camera and Microphone

Your browser will ask for permission to use your camera and microphone. You must allow both to proceed.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you accidentally block camera or microphone access, go to your browser's site settings to re-enable them, then refresh the page.
</Callout>

### Review the Question

Read the question prompt carefully. If the assessor included hints, they'll be shown before recording starts.

### Prepare During Think Time

A countdown timer gives you time to gather your thoughts before recording begins. This is typically 30 seconds to a few minutes, depending on how the assessment is configured.

### Record Your Response

When the countdown ends, recording starts automatically. Speak clearly and address the question directly.

- A timer shows how much recording time remains
- The maximum duration varies per question (typically 1-5 minutes)

### Review and Submit

If retakes are allowed, you can watch your recording and re-record if you're not satisfied. When you're ready, click **Submit** to send your response.

</Steps>

## After Submitting

Depending on the assessment settings, you may be able to see:

- **Your score** — An overall percentage based on how well your response matched the expected topics
- **Your transcript** — The AI-generated text of what you said, along with which keywords were detected

<Callout type="info">
  Not all assessments show results immediately. Some assessors review submissions manually before releasing scores.
</Callout>

## Tips for a Great Response

- **Find a quiet space** — Background noise can affect transcription accuracy
- **Good lighting** — Make sure your face is clearly visible
- **Speak naturally** — The AI transcribes conversational speech well; you don't need to speak slowly or unnaturally
- **Cover the key topics** — Think about what concepts the question is asking about and mention them specifically
- **Use your think time** — Don't rush. Use the preparation countdown to organize your main points
- **Check your setup** — Do a quick test of your camera and microphone before starting if possible

## Troubleshooting

### Camera or microphone not working
- Check that your browser has permission to access your camera and microphone
- Close other apps that might be using your camera (video calls, other browser tabs)
- Try a different browser (Chrome and Edge work best)

### Recording stops early
- Each question has a maximum recording time set by the assessor — this is normal
- Make sure your device doesn't go to sleep during recording

### Submission failed
- Check your internet connection and try again
- If the problem persists, contact the person who sent you the assessment

# Certificates & Badges

View, download, and share your earned certificates and digital badges.

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

## Overview

When you pass an assessment, you may earn a professional certificate and digital badge from the issuing organization. These credentials can be downloaded as PDFs, verified online, and shared on LinkedIn.

## Viewing Your Certificates

Navigate to **My Certificates** in your dashboard to see all certificates you've earned. Each certificate shows:

- **Assessment name** — What you completed
- **Issuing organization** — Who awarded the certificate
- **Date issued** — When you earned it
- **Score** — Your achievement score (if the issuer chose to display it)

## Downloading Certificates

Click **Download PDF** on any certificate to get a professionally formatted document you can:
- Print and frame
- Attach to job applications
- Include in your professional portfolio
- Send to your employer

## Sharing on LinkedIn

Click the **Share on LinkedIn** button to add your certificate directly to your LinkedIn profile. This creates a credential entry that includes:
- Certificate title
- Issuing organization
- Date earned
- Verification link

<Callout type="info">
  LinkedIn badge sharing is a one-click process. The verification link allows anyone viewing your LinkedIn profile to confirm your achievement is genuine.
</Callout>

## Verification

Every certificate includes a unique **verification link** and **QR code**. Share these with employers, recruiters, or anyone who needs to verify your credential. The verification page confirms:

- Your name
- The assessment completed
- The issuing organization
- The date of issue

## Tips

- **Share promptly** — Add certificates to LinkedIn while they're fresh for maximum visibility
- **Keep your profile updated** — Certificates showcase your commitment to continuous learning
- **Use the verification link** — Include it on your resume or portfolio for instant credibility

# Taking Assessments

Tips and guidance for completing self-paced assessments successfully.

import { Callout, Steps } from 'nextra/components'

## Before You Start

### Read the Instructions

Every assessment has specific instructions. Before beginning:
- Note the time limit (if any)
- Check if you can pause and resume
- Understand the question format
- Know if you can review/change answers

### Prepare Your Environment

<Steps>

### Find a Quiet Space

Minimize distractions. Let others know you'll be unavailable.

### Check Your Tech

Ensure stable internet and a charged device.

### Have Materials Ready

If allowed, gather any permitted reference materials.

### Clear Your Schedule

Allocate more time than the stated duration for buffer.

</Steps>

## During the Assessment

### Time Management

<Callout type="info">
  Don't spend too long on any single question. Mark difficult ones for review and move on.
</Callout>

- Watch the timer, but don't obsess
- Pace yourself—divide time by questions
- Save time for review at the end
- If stuck, make your best guess and flag for review

### Answering Questions

#### Multiple Choice
- Read all options before selecting
- Eliminate obviously wrong answers
- Watch for "all of the above" or "none of the above"
- Trust your first instinct unless clearly wrong

#### Short Answer
- Keep answers concise and specific
- Check spelling of technical terms
- Answer exactly what's asked

#### Code Questions
- Read requirements carefully
- Test with provided examples first
- Consider edge cases
- Comment your approach if time permits

### Navigation

Most assessments allow you to:
- Move forward and backward between questions
- Mark questions for review
- See which questions are answered
- Jump to specific questions

## Submitting Your Assessment

### Before Final Submission

<Steps>

### Review Flagged Questions

Return to any questions you marked for review.

### Check for Blanks

Ensure you haven't accidentally skipped questions.

### Verify Answers

If time permits, review your responses.

### Submit Confidently

Click submit when ready—you've done your best!

</Steps>

### After Submission

Depending on settings, you may:
- See your score immediately
- Receive results via email
- Need to wait for manual review
- Get a completion certificate

## Proctored Assessments

Some assessments include proctoring. If enabled:

<Callout type="warning">
  Proctored sessions record your webcam, screen, and browser activity. Ensure you're in a private, well-lit space.
</Callout>

### Proctoring Requirements
- Working webcam
- Microphone access
- Screen sharing permission
- Distraction-free environment

### Proctoring Rules
- Keep your face visible at all times
- Don't leave the camera view
- Avoid talking or looking away frequently
- No additional devices or people

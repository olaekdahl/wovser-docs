## What You'll Experience

### Live Quiz Sessions
Compete in real-time with other participants. Answer questions quickly and accurately to climb the leaderboard.

### Self-Paced Assessments
Complete evaluations on your own schedule within a given time window. Take your time to showcase your knowledge.

### Code Challenges
Demonstrate your programming skills in a browser-based coding environment with real terminal access.

## Your Dashboard

After logging in, your dashboard shows:
- **Upcoming Sessions** - Scheduled quizzes and assessments
- **In Progress** - Assessments you've started
- **Completed** - Your history and results
- **Certificates** - Earned credentials

<Callout type="info">
  Tip: Check your dashboard regularly for new invitations and upcoming deadlines!
</Callout>

## Account Settings

Customize your experience:
- **Profile** - Name, avatar, and bio
- **Notifications** - Email and in-app preferences
- **Accessibility** - Font size, contrast, and timing accommodations
- **Privacy** - Visibility and data preferences

# White-Label & Custom Domains

Make Wovser.AI look like your own platform with custom branding, themes, and domains.

## Overview

Enterprise customers can fully customize the Wovser.AI experience to match their brand identity. From custom domains to themed interfaces, your learners and candidates interact with a platform that feels like your own.

## Custom Domains

Host assessments on your own domain (e.g., `assessments.yourcompany.com`) instead of the default Wovser.AI URLs.

### How It Works

1. Navigate to **Organization Settings → White-Label → Custom Domain**
2. Enter your desired domain (e.g., `assessments.yourcompany.com`)
3. Add the provided DNS records to your domain registrar
4. The system automatically provisions an SSL certificate via Cloudflare
5. Once DNS propagates and the certificate is issued, your custom domain is active

<Callout type="warning">
  DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours. SSL certificate provisioning happens automatically once DNS is verified.
</Callout>

### What Changes

With a custom domain active:
- All assessment URLs use your domain
- Share links and QR codes point to your domain
- Email invitations reference your domain
- The browser address bar shows your brand

## Branding

Customize the visual identity of your Wovser.AI instance:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Company Name** | Displayed in headers, emails, and certificates |
| **Logo** | Your company logo (displayed in navigation and login) |
| **Logo Icon** | Compact icon version for mobile and small spaces |
| **Support Email** | Shown in help sections and error pages |
| **Support URL** | Link to your own help desk or documentation |

## Theme Customization

Apply your brand colors across the entire interface:

- **Primary Color** — Buttons, links, and accent elements
- **Secondary Color** — Supporting UI elements
- **Custom CSS** — Advanced styling for pixel-perfect control

## Hiding Wovser.AI Branding

Enterprise customers can optionally:
- **Hide "Powered by Wovser.AI"** — Remove the attribution footer
- **Hide Wovser branding** — Remove all Wovser.AI references from the interface

## Legal Links

Add your own legal documents:
- **Privacy Policy URL** — Link to your organization's privacy policy
- **Terms of Service URL** — Link to your terms of service

These replace the default Wovser.AI legal links throughout the platform.

## Tips

- **Set up early** — Custom domains take time to propagate, so configure this before launching assessments
- **Test thoroughly** — Verify all links work correctly with your custom domain
- **Upload high-quality logos** — Use SVG or high-resolution PNG for crisp display on all devices
- **Match your brand guide** — Use your exact brand colors for a seamless experience

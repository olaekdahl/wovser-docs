# Certificates & Badges

Award professional certificates and digital badges to learners who complete assessments.

import { Callout, Steps } from 'nextra/components'

## Overview

Certificates and badges let you formally recognize learner achievements. When a participant passes an assessment, quiz, or code challenge, they can receive a branded certificate PDF and a digital badge they can share on LinkedIn or embed on their website.

## Certificate Templates

Each organization can create custom certificate templates to match their brand identity.

### Template Layouts

| Layout | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Classic** | Traditional certificate design with formal borders and serif fonts |
| **Modern** | Clean, contemporary layout with bold typography |
| **Minimal** | Simple, elegant design with plenty of whitespace |
| **Professional** | Corporate-style with structured sections and logos |
| **Academic** | University-inspired design with crests and formal structure |

### Customization Options

Templates support extensive customization:

- **Colors** — Primary, secondary, background, and text colors
- **Logo** — Upload your organization's logo
- **Header & Footer Text** — Custom headings and taglines
- **Signature** — Signatory name, title, and uploaded signature image
- **Score Display** — Optionally show the learner's score on the certificate
- **Date & Issuer** — Toggle visibility of issue date and issuing organization
- **QR Code** — Include a scannable QR code for instant verification
- **Custom CSS** — Advanced styling for pixel-perfect branding

<Callout type="info">
  Each organization can have multiple templates and set one as default. You can assign different templates to different assessments.
</Callout>

## Digital Badges

Badges are visual icons that represent specific achievements. They appear alongside certificates and can be shared independently.

### Badge Shapes

| Shape | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| **Circle** | General certifications |
| **Shield** | Security and compliance credentials |
| **Hexagon** | Technical skill badges |
| **Square** | Course completion badges |
| **Ribbon** | Award and recognition badges |

### Badge Customization

- **Color** — Match your brand palette
- **Icon** — Upload a custom badge icon
- **Shape** — Choose from five professional shapes

## Creating a Certificate Template

<Steps>

### Navigate to Settings

Go to **Organization Settings → Certificates** in your dashboard.

### Create New Template

Click **New Template** and configure:
- Template name and description
- Choose a layout (Classic, Modern, Minimal, Professional, or Academic)
- Set brand colors and upload your logo
- Add signatory details and signature image
- Configure which information to display

### Preview and Save

Use the preview to see how the certificate will look with sample data, then save. Optionally set it as the default template for all new assessments.

</Steps>

## How Certificates Are Awarded

Certificates are automatically generated when a learner:
1. Completes an assessment, quiz, or code challenge
2. Meets or exceeds the **passing score** threshold
3. The assessment has certificate awarding enabled

Hosts can also manually award certificates to specific learners.

## Learner Experience

When a learner earns a certificate:
- They see a notification in their dashboard
- They can **download the PDF** certificate
- They can **share to LinkedIn** with a single click using the LinkedIn badge integration
- They receive a **verification URL** with QR code that third parties can use to confirm authenticity

## Verification

Each certificate includes a unique verification link and QR code. Anyone with the link can verify:
- The learner's name
- The assessment title
- The date issued
- The issuing organization
- The score achieved (if enabled)

<Callout type="warning">
  Certificate verification links are permanent and publicly accessible. They do not expose any other learner data beyond what is shown on the certificate itself.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **Keep branding consistent** — Use the same template across all assessments for a professional look
- **Add your signature** — Certificates with a real signature carry more weight
- **Enable QR codes** — Makes verification instant for hiring managers
- **Use LinkedIn integration** — Encourages learners to share achievements, increasing your brand visibility

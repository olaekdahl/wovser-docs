## Key Features

### Interactive Quiz Sessions
Host real-time quiz sessions where participants compete live. Perfect for team building, training assessments, or educational environments.

### Technical Assessments
Create comprehensive skill assessments with multiple question types, time limits, and automated scoring.

### Live Coding Challenges
Evaluate programming skills with browser-based coding environments. Supports multiple languages including JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, and more.

### AI-Powered Question Generation
Leverage AI to generate relevant questions based on your requirements, saving time while maintaining quality.

### Enterprise Integrations
Connect with your existing LMS (Canvas, Moodle), SSO providers (SAML, OIDC), and HR systems via SCIM.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

<Check>A Wovser.AI account ([sign up here](https://app.wovser.ai/auth/register))</Check>
<Check>Access to a project (created during onboarding)</Check>

## Create Your First Quiz

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Quiz Builder">
    From your dashboard, click **Create New** → **Quiz**.
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/create-quiz-button.png" alt="Create Quiz Button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Describe Your Quiz">
    Enter a prompt describing the quiz you want to create. Be specific about:
    - Topic or subject area
    - Difficulty level
    - Number of questions
    
    **Example prompt:**
    ```
    Create a 10-question quiz about JavaScript fundamentals 
    for beginner developers. Include questions about variables, 
    functions, and array methods.
    ```
    
    <Tip>
      The more detailed your prompt, the better the AI-generated questions will be.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review & Edit Questions">
    Wovser.AI will generate questions based on your prompt. You can:
    - Edit question text and options
    - Adjust correct answers
    - Reorder questions
    - Add or remove questions
    - Regenerate individual questions
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/edit-questions.png" alt="Edit Questions" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Set your quiz preferences:
    
    | Setting | Description |
    |---------|-------------|
    | Time Limit | Per-question or total quiz time |
    | Shuffle | Randomize question and answer order |
    | Show Answers | Reveal correct answers after submission |
    | Passing Score | Minimum percentage to pass |
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Launch Your Quiz">
    Click **Start Session** to begin. You'll receive:
    - A **PIN code** for participants to join
    - A **share link** for direct access
    - A **QR code** for easy mobile joining
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/session-started.png" alt="Session Started" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite Participants

Share your quiz using any of these methods:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="PIN Code" icon="hashtag">
    Participants visit `app.wovser.ai/join` and enter the 6-digit PIN
  </Card>
  <Card title="Direct Link" icon="link">
    Share the unique URL via email, Slack, or Teams
  </Card>
  <Card title="QR Code" icon="qrcode">
    Display on screen for participants to scan
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Monitor Live Results

Once participants start answering:

- See the live leaderboard update
- Control pacing (advance questions manually or auto-advance)
